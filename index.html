Optical Link V3

Experimental screen-to-camera optical modem. V3 moves away from low-density multi-state cells and uses a 64×64 high-density grid, simple 4-shape × 4-colour symbols, timing tracks, native-resolution camera decoding, local locator tracking, and Reed–Solomon error correction.

1. V3 Sender

Generate the high-density 64×64 optical stream. The first V3 profile uses ~1 KB DATA chunks at 1 fps while the physical link is validated.

Open Sender

2. V3 Receiver

Low-resolution locator tracking plus a separate native-resolution 768×768 data warp. Timing phase and RS correction are measured live.
